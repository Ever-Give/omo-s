Tonight he’d suddenly felt Bikini’s energy spike, flare and almost sort of… Explode. He’d felt her body giving off a massive distress signal that could only mean SOMETHING was horribly wrong. Her chi had gone utterly ballistic, which had always meant that she was either in severe pain, extremely scared, or both.

And, Vegeta hadn’t felt her chi erupt THIS much since what Frieza had done to her in the moments leading up to him killing her… Her chi had only ever reacted this intensely when she was being literally tortured…

He had no idea what could be happening to her now to provoke the same response. There was nothing in the house he could think of that a Saiyan could accidentally injure themselves with that badly. He didn’t sense any chi signatures nearby that didn’t BELONG here…

The only thing he COULD think of was that they’d somehow missed an android in Dr. Gero’s lab years ago, and it had come here to exact some form of revenge. Androids didn’t have chi, so Vegeta wouldn’t be able to feel one if it was in the house. And, if it caught Bikini by complete surprise while she was sleeping in bed, it COULD manage to inflict a lot of pain onto her.

He continued to run, only paying attention to Bikini’s energy to make sure he was going the right way. It felt like she was around the front of the building, it felt like—

There was a shift in her chi, as if it had suddenly snapped and gone loose. Fluttery, almost. Had whatever was torturing her finally stopped?

Vegeta should…

Vegeta should still make sure…

… Because, if there WAS an android loose in the house, it would probably make an awful mess of everything, it would be best to put a stop to it. Yeah.

Vegeta made it to the lobby just as the door to the women’s restroom opened. He raised his hands, prepared to fight if necessary, but it was only Bikini and Trunks. Trunks was holding Bikini’s hand, and she was asking him if he felt any better now.

“A little…” Trunks said. “What about you? Does your back still hurt?”

Vegeta lowered his fists, a deep, trembling blast of cold shooting into his stomach and chest. He had the most awful feeling that he knew what Trunks had MEANT by that…

Her back had been hurting… They’d just been in the bathroom… What if she’d…

She didn’t deserve to feel that… No one deserved to feel that. Well, except for Frieza, maybe…

WHY had she felt that?!

“Bikini, Trunks,” Vegeta spoke.

Trunks jumped, hiding himself behind Bikini. “I didn’t watch the ghost movie, Dad! I swear!”

Trunks had to learn how to lie more convincingly, but Vegeta could deal with THAT later. He didn’t care if Trunks had scared himself senseless with some ridiculous Earthling film, he cared that Bikini may have just experienced… “Bikini, are you alright? I felt your chi raise earlier.”

“I’m okay,” Bikini said, looking down at her feet as her face heated up. It was pretty humiliating that her energy reacted that way when she had to pee… To the point that her father was worried.

“Even your back?” Trunks asked. “How’d you hurt it, anyway? Did you pull a muscle, or—“

“Trunks,” Vegeta interrupted, sensing Bikini’s embarrassment. “Go back to your room, I need to talk to your sister.”

“But, the ghost might—“

“NOW, Trunks!” Vegeta commanded.

Trunks yelped and rushed away. Apparently, the only thing more frightening than a ghost was a scolding from Vegeta.

Once he was gone, Vegeta looked back at his daughter. “Bikini—“

“I didn’t LET Trunks watch that movie,” Bikini told him right away. “I didn’t even know he’d seen it until he came to my room earlier.”

“That’s not my concern,” Vegeta said. “What’s this he said about ‘back pain’?”

“I don’t think I pulled a muscle,” Bikini said. “Honestly, I’m not sure what happened. It was really sudden, and then it went away when I… When I…” She bit down on her lip, trying to make her blush fade. “Um…”

“When you relieved yourself?”

“Y—Yeah, that…”

The cold in Vegeta’s body dropped another few degrees. “Where exactly WAS this pain?”

“Right beneath my ribs, I’m pretty sure,” Bikini said.

Vegeta thought his blood must have been frozen solid by now. “And… Did the pain feel like a punishment for every mistake you’ve ever made in your entire life?”

“… Yeah,” Bikini agreed. “I don’t think I’ve ever felt anything like that before. Why?”

That was good, at least. Vegeta’s heart stopped thundering quite as fretfully in his ears. Only to ramp back up again when he realized that he had to CONTINUE now. Had to… Explain his reasons for being so nervous, how he knew so much about the hurt Bikini had experienced. “O—Okay… Uh… S—So that feeling… It, um… It means… Basically… It means…”

Bikini waited. Sometimes, it was easier to just let Dad fumble his way through conversations. He got like this whenever he had to share anything personal, discuss his feelings, admit to anything that he preferred to keep hidden away. When he spoke of fighting, of his accomplishments, the words came easily. Anything that brought him the tiniest amount of shame was next to impossible. And, Dad had a lot of things he was ashamed of— Some of those were things Bikini couldn’t UNDERSTAND why anyone would be ashamed of.

“It… It means that… You… Held your… Your bladder for too long…” Dad mumbled at last. “You were… You were feeling pain in your kidney area because… Er… Things were… Overflowing… But… But, not in the direction they were SUPPOSED to overflow…”

Oh. That… Kind of made sense. Part of it, anyway. Bikini understood why her Dad knew right away what had happened to her. He must have gotten that pain a lot, since he literally couldn’t pee most of the time. His Problem didn’t stop his bladder from filling beyond its natural capacity, but it DID stop him from emptying it. So, if his body wasn’t able to ‘overflow’ down the correct pathway… The only other way his liquid could move was back UP towards where it had come from.

Ouch… Poor Dad!

But, Bikini’s body didn’t have that same issue. Well, not often, anyway. Not to the extent her father suffered. At worst, Bikini would freeze up in unusual situations, like trying to pee into a urinal while standing up, or DIRECTLY in front of another person without a stall door or bush for cover. And, even so, she’d always found ways to relax her body during those incidents. She hadn’t COMPLETELY locked down.

And, with that being the case, shouldn’t she have ‘overflowed’ correctly? She had been leaking, after all… Shouldn’t she have just exploded and made a puddle on the floor instead of exploding and having her back snap apart?

“But… I don’t get it,” she said. “If I was too full… Why didn’t I… Why didn’t I just, um… You know…”

Vegeta shut his eyes. He didn’t want to ask this next question, but he NEEDED the answer. He feared it, the possibility of what it might be caused his throat to constrict, but if Bikini was having the kind of trouble he thought she was, he HAD to know. If he knew, he could try to fix it. Somehow. “Have you been having… Any… Any difficulties… Um… Voiding? A—Any at all?”

“Any at all?”

“Y—Yes,” Vegeta said. “H—Have there been any recent incidents wh—where you needed to… Relieve yourself, b—but your body wouldn’t allow you to?”

“Uh, well…”

Vegeta heard blood rushing through his ears, accompanied by a distant ringing sensation that was soon replaced by the rapid thudding of his own heartbeat.

“There was one day where I had to use the restroom with Gohan because… Because we both couldn’t wait anymore. And, uh, there wasn’t, like, a door separating us, so I had some trouble getting started…”

Vegeta tried to calm down. He was dismayed to learn she’d BEEN in that kind of situation, and if he found out Kakarot’s brat hadn’t kept his eyes to himself where they belonged, he and the Galick Gun would have to make time for a late night ‘visit’… But, taking longer to relax and pee while in such close proximity to another was… Normal. The only person that WOULDN’T happen to would probably be Kakarot. It was normal. It was not The Problem.

“He did not observe you, did he?!”

“N—No,” Bikini said. “He wasn’t watching… It just felt weird doing that so close to him now that we’re older, I guess…”

“And, that is the only time you’ve struggled?”

“Er… No.”

Vegeta’s mouth went dry.

“There was this time Videl and I… Ugh, do I really have to say all this? It’s embarrassing!”

“I… I will never breathe a word of it to anyone,” Vegeta said. His stomach churned and a twinge of cold anxiety rolled through his chest. If anyone ever tried to make HIM disclose each and every time he’d found himself incapable of taking a piss, he’d…

Well, he’d honestly have no idea of where to START, but he’d also be so humiliated that he’d wish for a pit to open up beneath him, with a hand to drag him down to Hell, an experience he’d find to be much more pleasant.

“O—Okay, um… Videl and I… One day, we both really needed to pee at Gohan’s house and, um, we couldn’t wait on each other… So… Er… We had to find a way to… To use the toilet together. And, neither of us could start right away…”

The gears in Vegeta’s brain spun for a bit, then short-circuited as he tried to imagine how Bikini and Videl had even MANAGED to do that. He knew Earthling girls pretty much ONLY sat down to urinate. Saiyan women had usually done it standing up like the men, but since Bikini didn’t have any older, female Saiyans around to teach her how it was done, she would need to sit like an Earthling… Had there actually been enough room for them to both go without making a mess?!

Well… Well, regardless of all those questions, that was another perfectly UNDERSTANDABLE time to have a couple minutes of stage-fright. There was no way the two girls had pulled that off without physically TOUCHING one another in the process, NO ONE would be able to just… Let go automatically and pee when they were THAT close to someone else.

Vegeta released a breath. It didn’t sound like she had The Problem. “Is that it?”

Bikini covered her face, but Vegeta could still see the red ting of her blush. “Um… O—One more…”

Vegeta braced himself. She seemed more embarrassed by this NEXT incident than she’d been of the other two. What could possibly be more shameful than trying to share a toilet seat with another person?!

The Problem could be…

Her bladder completely sealing itself shut as she tried desperately to empty it— Empty it even just a little, just enough to take the edge off of a burning, excruciating bout of desperation…

She HAD experienced it to that degree once. She must have. It was the only thing he could think of that could make her feel so ashamed of herself that she could no longer look him in the eye as she spoke.

“Er…” Bikini turned away, not even facing Vegeta’s direction anymore. “Um… S—So, the last time, it was… I kinda… I had to go really bad, but… Um…”

‘But I couldn’t, no matter how hard I tried…’ Vegeta imagined her saying. ‘It hurt so much, and everyone was staring. They could tell I had to go, and didn’t know why I was holding it. I just know that they must have thought I was broken and weak.’

“But… But, the girls’ bathroom was closed,” Bikini said instead. “And, the men’s didn’t have a working stall, only urinals.”

Vegeta’s brows raised. “So… You…”

“So, er… Piccolo was there. He… He kinda… He got me a funnel…” Bikini shook her head. “Ah, I… I just— You know, I couldn’t wait anymore… And so, I… I had the funnel, and I went to the urinal to…. To go. But, it felt SO weird, and I was nervous I’d mess it up, and… I couldn’t go until Piccolo turned on the sink for me.”

Well…

That wasn’t The Problem.

Unlike the previous two stories, Vegeta couldn’t relate to it quite AS easily. He could imagine what it was like to try to pee with someone right there in the room with him, he could imagine the awkwardness of trying to share a toilet with another person. But he couldn’t imagine trying to urinate in a position that he’d been taught all his life was the wrong way to go, needing to quickly retrain his body and convince it that it would be alright.

Yet, if what Bulma said was correct and Earthling women barely EVER stood up while relieving themselves, maybe the majority of them would struggle the first time like Bikini had?

It was probably normal. Reasonable.

What he didn’t understand at all was why Bikini seemed so ashamed of herself for doing it. He peed standing up all the time… Not into urinals, of course, but still… What was the big deal?

“I… I know that was really weird,” Bikini said. “If I could have held it, I would—“

“How is it weird?” Vegeta asked. Yeah, it was unusual for females on Earth, but it baffled him that she seemed to think he’d be ANGRY at her over it. Hell, having seen the Saiyan women urinate while standing on numerous occasions, Vegeta had assumed for years that it was the norm across the universe. It wasn’t until the first time he had to take Bikini to a private spot to relieve herself on Namek that he learned Earthlings did it differently.

“It’s weird for girls to use urinals, or pee standing up at all,” Bikini said.

“It wasn’t on our home planet,” Vegeta said. “All Saiyans stood to go the majority of the time. Male and female.”

Something in Bikini seemed to relax. “Oh, so I was just acting more like a Saiyan.”

“Yes, exactly,” Vegeta said, calming down as well. She looked far less ashamed of herself now, certain that he wasn’t angry at her for relieving herself in an unconventional (to her) way. If anything, he was oddly proud that she’d managed to do it more like a typical Saiyan. And, now he was sure she wasn’t developing his Problem. She’d just had a few tricky situations to work her way through, and she’d managed to get herself empty each time.

But, that still left the question of why she’d experienced the back pain… If her bladder was so much more obedient than Vegeta’s, that shouldn’t have happe—

“I take it Trunks was with you earlier? When you felt… That sensation in your back?”

“Yeah. We both had to go, and he didn’t want to be left alone.”

“Perhaps THAT’S why your… Why your body reacted that way,” Vegeta said. “You DO always try to maintain your strength in his presence, particularly when he’s frightened.”

That WAS true. When their father died trying to destroy Buu, Bikini had done her best not to cry anywhere Trunks would see. She was the only one Trunks had left to protect him at that point, so he had to see her as indestructible, able to take on anything.

Had she done the same kind of thing tonight? Wanting to make sure Trunks knew she’d keep him safe from his imaginary ghost, she’d refused to give in to anything that would make her seem too weak for the task?

So, when her bladder got so full that she SHOULD have had an accident, her determination just… Hadn’t let it happen, and THAT was why it started to spray back up the wrong pipes?

“That makes sense, I guess,” Bikini said. “I doubt he’d think I could actually keep ghosts away if I’d… Um… Had an… If I’d… You know.”

“The pain is gone now?” Vegeta asked.

“Yeah, it went away completely.”

“Good. If it ever happens again, you are to come directly to me,” Vegeta told her. “Especially if you’d already attempted to use the restroom and weren’t able. If you experience anything like that, I must be informed.”

Bikini didn’t THINK she could completely lock up like Dad did, and even if it DID ever happen… “But… That would be so emba—“

“I know,” Vegeta said. “You come to me, because I… Because, you know this is something I could never judge anyone for, and…” He clenched his fists, trying to slow his rapid pulse as he shoved out the next words; “And… And I… Know how it works… Methods for… For dealing with it.”

“Okay,” Bikini said. She started for her room, but hesitated. “You’re not gonna tell Mom that I… Had that back-thing happen, right? I don’t want her to freak out.”

“I will not,” Vegeta said. “If I tell her ANYTHING about tonight, it will be in regards to how Trunks snuck around and watched that damn film.”
