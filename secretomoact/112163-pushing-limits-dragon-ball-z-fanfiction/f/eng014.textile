Bikini nodded meekly. She couldn’t believe the amount of help she had needed just to use the restroom! Gohan must have been surprised as well, considering how he wouldn’t even look at her. “Yeah… S—Sorry about that… And… For hitting the ceiling with my energy earlier…”

“You didn’t mean to…”

“But, everyone else can control that,” Bikini said.

“Your dad sparks like that when he gets too angry,” Gohan pointed out.

“Yeah, but he doesn’t go to school… Or anywhere else that it would be much of a problem, really,” Bikini groaned. “I wish I could just live like he does, then this stuff wouldn’t matter.”

“I think you’d get pretty lonely,” Gohan said. “Our dads can train alone for a really long time, but you don’t like it when you’ve got no one to talk to.”

Bikini sighed. Gohan was right. The full-blooded Saiyans she knew communicated more through actions than through words. That was the main reason she and her father had had so much trouble understanding each other. He was getting better at it, but he’d needed to be TAUGHT, it hadn’t come naturally to him at all.

And, she’d been convinced that Dad hated Goku because the way he spoke made it SOUND like he did, she hadn’t looked beneath the surface to examine his actual behavior until she’d been prompted to.

“It’s like I’m too much of a human to be a Saiyan, and too much of a Saiyan to be a human,” Bikini said.

“A—At least you’re not the only one like that, right?” Gohan said.

“Guess so,” Bikini said. And, even though she wasn’t sure if it was the best idea to do this when she was in heat, she went over to Gohan and hugged him.

Gohan froze, startled. He shouldn’t have been, Bikini loved to be hugged, but— Why now? Why now? Now, when it felt like most of his blood had rushed to the last place he wanted it to go?!

Bikini held onto him for several seconds before the comment he’d been dreading emerged. “Hm? Something’s poking me, what’s—“

“Nothing— Nothing! I, uh— Must have forgotten something in my pocket— I— I— Actually, I have to pee now too all of the sudden! Do you mind if I do it in here?”

“Go ahead, I gue—“

“THANKS!” Gohan ran into the closest stall.
