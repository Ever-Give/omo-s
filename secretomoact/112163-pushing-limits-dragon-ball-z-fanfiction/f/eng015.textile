The bottles were still there. Vegeta saw her vision drawn straight towards them, and it lingered there. Heat scorched all around him, his face and neck burning so brightly it felt like he’d caught fire. His brain scrambled, thoughts jumbling together in search of an explanation— any explanation— that didn’t involve him getting so desperate that he’d peed in the bottles.

“M—Mom, I’m sorry!” Bikini blurted out. “I know, it’s gross! But, we were in there for so long, and Dad said it was okay if I used the bottles!”

“It’s s—so embarrassing!” Bikini said. “I had to use a funnel so I didn’t make a mess, and Dad had to help me, a—and— Ugh, I had to go so bad that I needed SIX bottles! That’s SO much! I’m so gross! Please, don’t tell anyone!”
